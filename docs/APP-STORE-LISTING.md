**Description** [≤4000]

> LuminaVault is one vault for everything you mean to come back to: notes, links, photos, files and voice memos. Ask a question and its agent answers from your vault, showing you which note it used so you can check.
>
> SAVE IT ONCE
> • Type, paste a link or attach a photo or file from Home.
> • Share from Safari, Photos or any app with the share sheet.
> • Record a voice note; it is transcribed and saved as text.
> • Bring existing markdown notes by importing a folder.
>
> ASK YOUR VAULT
> • Ask in plain words and get an answer drawn from your own notes.
> • Every answer shows the notes it used, so you can open them and check.
> • Answers come from AI models and can be wrong. Check anything that matters.
>
> SEE HOW IT CONNECTS
> • Brain draws your notes and their links as a graph you can explore.
> • Spaces keep work, reading, trips and the rest apart.
>
> AN AGENT YOU SHAPE
> • Its personality is a plain-text file, SOUL.md, that you write and edit.
> • Suggested changes wait for your approval.
>
> USE IT FROM YOUR CODING AGENT
> • Connect Claude Code, Codex or another MCP client to search and read your vault.
>
> YOUR DATA
> • Export your whole vault as plain markdown at any time.
> • Delete your account from the app.
> • Before anything is sent to an AI provider, the app tells you which ones and asks first.
>
> LuminaVault also runs on the web at app.luminavault.fyi.
